# V0.2 End-to-End Test Checklist

Run this checklist before changing realtime translation behavior. Do not print, copy, screenshot, or commit API key values.

## Startup

- Run `npm run dev:all` from the project root.
- Open `http://127.0.0.1:3000/`.
- Confirm the frontend loads.
- Confirm the Realtime Proxy is listening on `ws://127.0.0.1:3001`.
- Confirm the status panel shows `未开始`.

## Japanese Mode

- Select `日本語`.
- Click `开始同传`.
- Confirm the status panel changes to listening or connecting, then `正在听对方`.
- Speak Japanese as the other speaker.
- Confirm the source subtitle is readable.
- Confirm the Chinese translation appears in realtime.
- Hold `按住说中文`, speak Chinese, then release.
- Confirm the button shows `松开结束并翻译` while held.
- Confirm Japanese text appears.
- Confirm Japanese audio plays.
- Tap `重新播放` and confirm audio can replay.

## English Mode

- Select `English`.
- Click `开始同传`.
- Confirm the status panel changes to listening or connecting, then `正在听对方`.
- Speak English as the other speaker.
- Confirm the source subtitle is readable.
- Confirm the Chinese translation appears in realtime.
- Hold `按住说中文`, speak Chinese, then release.
- Confirm the button shows `松开结束并翻译` while held.
- Confirm English text appears.
- Confirm English audio plays.
- Tap `重新播放` and confirm audio can replay.

## Switching

- While idle, switch between `日本語` and `English`.
- While listening, switch between `日本語` and `English`.
- Confirm the header and target output card update.
- Confirm the next Chinese push-to-talk turn uses the selected target language.
- Confirm switching is disabled while recording, translating, or playing.

## Controls

- Confirm `按住说中文` is visible before clicking `开始同传`.
- From a fresh page load, hold `按住说中文` and confirm the app starts automatically.
- Confirm releasing `按住说中文` submits the Chinese turn.
- Confirm `静音` stops automatic playback but keeps subtitles working.
- Confirm `恢复听译` clears the current turn and returns to listening mode.
- Confirm `清空字幕` clears current captions and history.
- Confirm `停止同传` returns the app to idle.

## Recovery

- During Chinese recording, click `恢复听译` and confirm the app returns to listening mode.
- During translation or playback, click `恢复听译` and confirm playback stops.
- After recovery, hold `按住说中文` again and confirm a new Chinese turn works.
- Stop and start again, then confirm both listening and Chinese push-to-talk still work.

## Translation Quality Samples

Use short, natural sentences first, then test longer spoken sentences.

- Chinese: `我现在准备去车站，可能会晚到十分钟。`
- Chinese: `这句话不是批评你，我只是想确认一下你的意思。`
- Chinese: `这个方案可以，但预算和时间需要再调整。`
- Japanese: `今から駅に向かうので、10分ほど遅れるかもしれません。`
- Japanese: `責めているわけではなく、意味を確認したいだけです。`
- English: `I'm heading to the station now, so I may be about ten minutes late.`
- English: `I'm not criticizing you. I just want to make sure I understood what you meant.`

## Pass Criteria

- Both services stay running.
- No API key value appears anywhere in terminal output or UI.
- Status panel reflects the current mode.
- The app recovers after `恢复听译`.
- The app recovers after stopping and starting again.
- Source subtitles do not lose the main meaning of a sentence.
- Translations preserve intent, tone, and negation.
